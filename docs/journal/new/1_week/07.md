### Conflict discovery

Tried to check whether the branch would merge into `main`. Found
43-vs-15 divergence: `main` had 43 commits we didn't have, including
the entire NVIDIA NIM pipeline (PR #28), LangGraph parallel runner,
agents, prompts, CI workflow, and a *second* `app/core/config.py`
module. Most importantly: `main` already had a complete data layer
under **different column names** (named PKs like `manager_id`/
`morning_note_id`, `portfolios` as M:M join table, `Vector(1536)`
embedding, `Enum`-based status, `ARRAY(JSONB)` nullable flags, RLS
using `NULLIF(... , '')::integer`).

Our branch's design was incompatible at the column-name level, not
just additive. A normal merge would have produced days of conflict
resolution on a foundation we were intentionally rebuilding.

### Architectural decision: rebuild posture

Re-read `docs/issues.md`. It treats issue #03 as the actual first
coding issue. Everything before it (#01 repo setup, #02 docker
compose) is scaffolding; everything from #03 onward is the build.
`main` was a historical artifact from the previous attempt.

Decision: **discard everything in `main`.** Our branch is the new
foundation. PR #30 doesn't merge into `main`; it replaces `main`.
